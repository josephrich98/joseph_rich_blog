description: "A standardized, reproducible pipeline for turning heterogeneous multi-institutional CT data (e.g., from The Cancer Imaging Archive) into analysis-ready volumes for radiomics and deep learning. The notebook-based workflow handles series filtering, DICOM-to-NIfTI conversion, orientation and voxel-spacing harmonization, intensity normalization, and metadata validation, reducing the non-biological variability across scanners and protocols that otherwise destabilizes radiomic features and model performance; it is demonstrated on TCGA-KIRC renal-cancer scans."
