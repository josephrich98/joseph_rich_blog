description: "A cost-effectiveness modeling study of AI-assisted imaging for small renal masses, using a Markov microsimulation of 10,000 patients over a 10-year horizon to compare standard of care, an MRI+AI workflow, and two CT+AI workflows. The CT-based AI strategies yielded the highest quality-adjusted life years at the lowest cost — dominating standard of care and MRI+AI — suggesting AI risk stratification could reduce overtreatment cost-effectively; as a decision-analytic simulation, the findings are economic projections that motivate, rather than substitute for, prospective clinical validation."
