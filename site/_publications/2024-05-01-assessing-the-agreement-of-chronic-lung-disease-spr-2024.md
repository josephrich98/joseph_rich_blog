description: "Presented at the Society for Pediatric Radiology 2024 annual meeting: a study of 95 preterm infants comparing radiologists' chest X-ray diagnoses of chronic lung disease of prematurity against several standard clinical definitions, finding over 90% agreement and that radiologists often diagnose the condition, in the more severely affected infants, before the 36-week postmenstrual-age milestone those definitions use. Published in <i>Maternal Health, Neonatology and Perinatology</i>."
