description: "RADAR, a multimodal agentic assistant that pairs a large language model with CT image-analysis tools and retrieval-augmented generation to answer renal mass assessment and management questions (surveillance, biopsy, ablation, partial or radical nephrectomy) with its measurements and reasoning. On a 418-item benchmark built from 20 KiTS23 cases, RADAR with a Qwen2.5-7B model selected the reference answer in 80.6% of items, versus 63.4% for GPT-4o given lesion-centered slices."
