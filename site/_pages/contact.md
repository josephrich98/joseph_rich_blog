## Work with me

I'm open to collaborating and consulting on projects involving artificial intelligence, medical imaging, computational biology, and applied mathematics. As a physician-scientist in training, I have worked on projects that involve both framing a clinical question as a computational problem and building the software and mathematical infrastructure to bring the idea to life. Below are the kinds of projects I take on, with examples of past work.

**AI model development.** Designing, training, and clinically evaluating imaging and multimodal models.

- [RADAR]({{ '/publications/2026-12-12-radar-an-agentic-assistant-for-renal-mass-assessment' | relative_url }}), an agentic assistant that answers renal mass management questions from CT and the clinical record
- FVA, a method for naming the axes of a learned latent space with interpretable features
- [Clinical evaluation of imputed CT images]({{ '/publications/2025-08-13-image-imputation-with-conditional-generative-adversarial-networks-captures' | relative_url }}) generated by conditional GANs
- Tumor segmentation of [osseous metastatic prostate cancer]({{ '/publications/2025-02-06-deep-learning-based-detection-and-segmentation-of-osseous' | relative_url }}) on CT, including [foundation model benchmarking]({{ '/publications/2025-11-21-analyzing-foundation-models-for-segmentation-of-osseous-metastatic' | relative_url }})

**Bioinformatics workflows.** End-to-end analysis of sequencing data, from raw reads to biological interpretation.

- Single-cell analysis of RNA communication between cells
- Single-cell analysis of CAR T cell therapy
- [varseek]({{ '/publications/2025-09-02-reference-based-variant-detection-with-varseek' | relative_url }}), reference-based variant detection in RNA-seq
- [cellsweep]({{ '/publications/2026-03-06-single-cell-genomics-decontamination-with-cellsweep' | relative_url }}), decontamination of single-cell genomics data

**Reproducibility.** Auditing whether a result survives a change of software, preprocessing, or cohort.

- [Seurat vs. Scanpy]({{ '/publications/2026-03-27-the-impact-of-package-selection-and-versioning-on' | relative_url }}): how package selection and versioning change single-cell RNA-seq results
- [Preprocessing choices in single-cell foundation models]({{ '/publications/2026-12-12-the-effect-of-preprocessing-choices-on-single-cell' | relative_url }})
- [ctkit](https://github.com/pachterlab/ctkit), a reproducible protocol for turning TCIA radiology studies into AI-ready arrays
- [External validation of nnU-Net]({{ '/publications/2025-07-15-evaluation-of-nnu-net-for-kidney-tumor-segmentation' | relative_url }}) for kidney tumor segmentation (KiTS23) on a large independent cohort

**AI and science education.** Explaining AI to clinicians and science to students.

- Radiology AI grand rounds at Children's Hospital Los Angeles (CHLA)
- Radiology AI grand rounds at the Keck School of Medicine of USC
- MCAT tutoring

**Open-source academic software.** Tools that take friction out of research.

- [paperpush](https://github.com/pachterlab/paperpush), which automates academic submission portals
- [BioCartoon](https://www.biocartoon.com), a free biology figure editor built on 15,000+ open-licensed illustrations
- manimble
- [Conference Agent](https://conferenceagent.vercel.app), a searchable table of conference deadlines across medicine, genomics, and data science

**Applied mathematics.** Turning a vague modeling question into a precise, provable one.

- [Radiogenomics theory]({{ '/publications/2026-11-29-an-upper-bound-on-genomic-recoverability-from-computed-tomography' | relative_url }}): an upper bound on how much genomic information any model can recover from CT
- Axiomatic PCA
- The genome alignment polytope

If any of this overlaps with what you're working on, [email me](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}) with a short description of the project and we can discuss more details.
